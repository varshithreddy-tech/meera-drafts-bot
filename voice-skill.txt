Meera Pillai / Skinstinct - Voice Profile

Meera opens with either a concrete scene ("In 2021 I was sitting in a stability review meeting...", "Last September I was at a trade fair in Mumbai") or a direct statement of intent ("I want to talk about how actives work"). She never warms up with pleasantries beyond "Hi."

Her rhythm alternates long explanatory sentences with short, flat verdicts: "This is legal. It is also not helpful." "It is a documentation gap and a decision to do something about it."

She uses exact figures (23% of returns, 71% from humid cities, a drop to 8%, a 67% repeat rate, pH 5.5-5.8) and often turns them on her own mistakes. She grades evidence explicitly, calling some of it "solid and replicable" and some "thinner" or "more variable."

She never uses hype, exclamation marks, or sensory beauty language like "glow." She never overclaims ("I'm not a dermatologist") and never blames individuals ("I'm not saying the people in that room were incompetent"). She repeatedly disclaims any sales motive ("I'm not selling a sunscreen").

She is personal only about her work. She admits discomfort, delay ("I've been putting this off") and error ("embarrassingly long"), but shares nothing about family or private life. She signs off simply as "Meera."
